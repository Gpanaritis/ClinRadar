How it works

From an event abroad to an approved shipment plan.

  1. 1

    Watch

    Monitors only the countries where a trial is active, as recorded in your CTMS: strikes and transport, weather and natural hazards, security and travel advice, regulatory and customs change.

  2. 2

    Check

    Tests each planned shipment, leg by leg and day by day, against events on its route and the destination's rules: import permits, labels, importer of record, delivery days.

  3. 3

    Plan

    When the courier or depot confirms a disruption, AI agents propose recovery options: another departure airport, another dispatch date, stock from another depot.

  4. 4

    Verify

    Fixed rules check every option. Does it arrive before the site runs out of stock? Is the lane qualified for the temperature range? Do the import permit terms still hold?

  5. 5

    Approve

    Clinical Supply approves one plan. Changes reach IRT, ERP, the depot and the courier as drafts that people approve in those systems.

Example Illustrative scenario

A strike day in Belgium cancels a flight to Tel Aviv.

Patients on treatment at the site
14
Site stock runs out
27 Nov
Delivery if nobody acts
29 Nov
Delivery with the approved plan
26 Nov

  1. The signal

    The airport and the federal transport authority announce a national strike day on 24 Nov. ClinRadar merges both notices into one signal for Belgium.

  2. The impact

    The courier cancels the Brussels to Tel Aviv flight of shipment SHP-IL-01. Its next free flight would deliver on 29 Nov, two days after the site runs out: 14 patients would go 2 days without treatment.

  3. The options

    The agents propose three. Leaving from Amsterdam passes every rule, with the same delivery day. Dispatching on 25 Nov fails: the kits arrive after the stock runs out. Leaving from Luxembourg needs a monitoring decision first.

  4. The decision

    Clinical Supply approves the Amsterdam plan. The new courier booking and the depot instruction go out as drafts for approval in those systems.

Validation

Designed to support your validation.

ClinRadar gives Quality the records it needs to assess the system. It does not replace your validated systems.

Trace the source

Every signal and rule shows its source passage, version and date.

Reproduce the checks

Fixed rules, not the AI, decide pass or fail. The same inputs give the same result.

Record who decided and why

Role-based approval. Decisions, reasons and agent runs go into a hash-chained audit trail.

Execute in validated systems

Nothing is written straight into IRT, ERP or the QMS. Changes arrive there as drafts that people approve.

Each company validates ClinRadar for its own intended use, for example under GAMP 5, 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11.
